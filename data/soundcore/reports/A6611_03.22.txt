  0481 00 00 df   left double tap: off (F)                         state byte 53: ff
  0481 00 00 fd   left double tap: back to change mode             state byte 53: dd
  0481 01 00 62   right double tap: previous (2)                   state byte 54: 22
  0481 01 00 26   right double tap: back to play/pause (6)         state byte 54: 66
  0481 01 05 0d   right triple tap: change mode                    state byte 56: dd
  0481 01 05 d0   right triple tap: back to volume up (0)          state byte 56: 00
  The app writes the byte's high four bits as the state had them and the new action in the low four; the
  earbuds store the low four bits in both halves.
  1503            reads the sleep timer: 02 78 00 01 (state bytes 67-70)
  1585 01 78 00 01   sleep timer on, 120 minutes
  1585 01 1e 00 01   30 minutes
  1585 02 1e 00 01   off, keeping 30 minutes
  0283            13 EQ writes: Acoustic (1), Pop (15), Classical (4), Rock (17), Bass Booster (2), then a custom
                  curve band by band (FE FE). Each sends the preset, one curve of 10 bytes (bands 9 and 10:
                  78 00 for a preset, 78 78 for the custom curve) and that curve after OpenSCQ30's correction;
                  every correction matches PairFree's (SoundcoreEq.drc).
  0103            09 09 08: left, right, case, in tenths (offset 1)
  01A6            10 bytes the app sends now and then: the phone's time (seconds since 1970, little-endian,
                  then six more bytes)
  1401            the alarm list, empty here; 1503/1507/1506 sleep settings and logs; 0503/0583 log transfer
  01A9 01 / 00    unknown: the earbuds answer 0114 01 / 00 and 1303 (11 bytes, the tenth 01 / 00)
