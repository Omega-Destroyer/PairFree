PairFree is a free, open-source Android app that controls 1MORE earbuds and headphones without 1MORE's app: no account, no internet access.

PairFree does not need 1MORE's own app to run, but only one app can hold the control connection to the earbuds at a time, so close 1MORE's own app first.

## What you can control

Each model shows only the settings it has:

- Battery for each earbud and the case, and noise control: the kind of noise cancelling and of transparency.
- The equalizer: the model's preset sounds and, on models with one, a curve of your own; spatial audio.
- What a double and a triple tap do, two devices at once, and the connection mode.
- The NANO's light, and finding a lost earbud.

## 1MORE models

PairFree knows 39 1MORE models. No model of this brand has been tried on real earbuds with PairFree yet; support comes from the brand's own app and reports.

1MORE support is read from 1MORE's own app. It has not been tried with PairFree yet.

The [full model list for 1MORE on GitHub](https://github.com/Omega-Destroyer/PairFree/blob/main/docs/devices/1more.md) shows what works on each one. If your model is not marked as tried, a device report from you adds it: see [how to help](https://github.com/Omega-Destroyer/PairFree#help-add-your-model).

## Why use PairFree

- **No account and no internet.** The app has no internet permission, so it cannot go online.
- **One app for several brands.** The same screens for every brand, instead of one app per pair of earbuds.
- **Open source.** GPL-3.0; the protocol is documented in the repository with tests, so anyone can check what the app sends to the earbuds.
- **Small and quick,** about 3 MB, and reproducible: the published APK can be rebuilt from the source.

## Get PairFree

PairFree is on [F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/io.github.omegadestroyer.pairfree/) and on the [GitHub releases page](https://github.com/Omega-Destroyer/PairFree/releases/latest). It needs Android 12 or later.
