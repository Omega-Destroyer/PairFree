PairFree is a free, open-source Android and Windows app that controls 1MORE earbuds and headphones without 1MORE's app: no account, no internet access.

PairFree does not need 1MORE's own app to run, but only one app can hold the control connection to the earbuds at a time, so close 1MORE's own app first.

## What you can control

Each model shows only the settings it has:

- Battery for each earbud and the case, and noise control: the kind of noise cancelling and of transparency.
- The equalizer: the model's preset sounds and, on models with one, a curve of your own; spatial audio.
- What a double and a triple tap do, two devices at once, and the connection mode.
- The NANO's light, and finding a lost earbud.

## On Windows too

Almost none of these brands makes a Windows app, so on a PC their earbuds are plain Bluetooth audio. PairFree for Windows is one small portable program (no installer) with the same settings as the Android app, in a window made for a mouse and with a tray icon for the battery and quick switches. It needs Windows 10 (version 1809) or 11, 64-bit, with the earbuds paired in Windows' Bluetooth settings. 1MORE is covered.

## 1MORE models

PairFree knows 39 1MORE models. No model of this brand has been tried on real earbuds with PairFree yet; support comes from the brand's own app and reports.

1MORE support is read from 1MORE's own app. It has not been tried with PairFree yet.

If your model is not marked as tried, a device report from you adds it: see [how to help](https://github.com/Omega-Destroyer/PairFree#help-add-your-model).

## Why use PairFree

- **No account and no internet.** The app has no internet permission, so it cannot go online.
- **One app for several brands.** The same screens for every brand, instead of one app per pair of earbuds.
- **Open source.** GPL-3.0; the protocol is documented in the repository with tests, so anyone can check what the app sends to the earbuds.
- **Small and quick:** about 6 MB on Android and 16 MB on Windows, and reproducible: the published APK can be rebuilt from the source.
- **On Windows as well as Android,** where most of these brands have no app at all.

## Get PairFree

On Android, PairFree is on [F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/io.github.omegadestroyer.pairfree/) and on the [GitHub releases page](https://github.com/Omega-Destroyer/PairFree/releases/latest); it needs Android 12 or later. On Windows 10 or 11, download `PairFree.exe` from the same releases page and run it.
