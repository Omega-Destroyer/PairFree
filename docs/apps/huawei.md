PairFree is a free, open-source Android and Windows app that controls Huawei FreeBuds, FreeClip, FreeLace, Eyewear and Honor earbuds without HUAWEI AI Life: no account, no internet access.

PairFree does not need HUAWEI Audio Connect (formerly AI Life) to run, but only one app can hold the control connection to the earbuds at a time, so close HUAWEI Audio Connect (formerly AI Life) first.

## What you can control

Each model shows only the settings it has:

- Battery for each earbud and the case, and noise control with its levels where the model has it.
- The equalizer: the earbuds' own sounds, your own curves where the model keeps them, and the saved ones.
- Touch, pinch and swipe controls, wear detection, low latency, two devices at once and the paired devices.
- The newer models' spatial audio, head gestures, find my earbuds, rest reminders and case prompt sounds.

## On Windows too

Almost none of these brands makes a Windows app, so on a PC their earbuds are plain Bluetooth audio. PairFree for Windows is one small portable program (no installer) with the same settings as the Android app, in a window made for a mouse and with a tray icon for the battery and quick switches. It needs Windows 10 (version 1809) or 11, 64-bit, with the earbuds paired in Windows' Bluetooth settings. Huawei and Honor is covered.

## Huawei and Honor models

PairFree knows 52 Huawei and Honor models. No model of this brand has been tried on real earbuds with PairFree yet; support comes from the brand's own app and reports.

Huawei support is read from Huawei's own apps (HUAWEI Audio Connect 2.0.7.390, and AI Life 11.0.2.305 for the oldest models), checked against LibreBuds, HuaweiPods and OpenFreebuds. It has not been tried with PairFree on any product yet.

If your model is not marked as tried, a device report from you adds it: see [how to help](https://github.com/Omega-Destroyer/PairFree#help-add-your-model).

## Why use PairFree

- **No account and no internet.** The app has no internet permission, so it cannot go online.
- **One app for several brands.** The same screens for every brand, instead of one app per pair of earbuds.
- **Open source.** GPL-3.0; the protocol is documented in the repository with tests, so anyone can check what the app sends to the earbuds.
- **Small and quick:** about 6 MB on Android and 16 MB on Windows, and reproducible: the published APK can be rebuilt from the source.
- **On Windows as well as Android,** where most of these brands have no app at all.

## Get PairFree

On Android, PairFree is on [F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/io.github.omegadestroyer.pairfree/) and on the [GitHub releases page](https://github.com/Omega-Destroyer/PairFree/releases/latest); it needs Android 12 or later. On Windows 10 or 11, download `PairFree.exe` from the same releases page and run it.
