PairFree is a free, open-source Android app that controls Jabra's Elite and Evolve2 earbuds and headphones that speak Jabra's own protocol without Sound+: no account, no internet access.

PairFree does not need Jabra Sound+ to run, but only one app can hold the control connection to the earbuds at a time, so close Jabra Sound+ first.

## What you can control

Each model shows only the settings it has:

- Battery for each earbud and the case.
- Noise control (off, noise cancelling, HearThrough) with its levels, and the modes the button cycles through.
- The equalizer with Sound+'s presets, the button functions, sidetone and the voice prompt language.

## Jabra models

PairFree knows 27 Jabra models. No model of this brand has been tried on real earbuds with PairFree yet; support comes from the brand's own app and reports.

Jabra support is read from Jabra Sound+ 5.22.0. It has not been tried with PairFree on any product yet. The models Sound+ drives through Qualcomm's GAIA (Elite 2, 3, 4, 5 and Connect 5t) are not covered yet.

The [full model list for Jabra on GitHub](https://github.com/Omega-Destroyer/PairFree/blob/main/docs/devices/jabra.md) shows what works on each one. If your model is not marked as tried, a device report from you adds it: see [how to help](https://github.com/Omega-Destroyer/PairFree#help-add-your-model).

## Why use PairFree

- **No account and no internet.** The app has no internet permission, so it cannot go online.
- **One app for several brands.** The same screens for every brand, instead of one app per pair of earbuds.
- **Open source.** GPL-3.0; the protocol is documented in the repository with tests, so anyone can check what the app sends to the earbuds.
- **Small and quick,** about 3 MB, and reproducible: the published APK can be rebuilt from the source.

## Get PairFree

PairFree is on [F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/io.github.omegadestroyer.pairfree/) and on the [GitHub releases page](https://github.com/Omega-Destroyer/PairFree/releases/latest). It needs Android 12 or later.
