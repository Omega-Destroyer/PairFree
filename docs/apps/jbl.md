PairFree is a free, open-source Android app that controls JBL earbuds and headphones without the JBL Headphones app: no account, no internet access.

PairFree does not need the JBL Headphones app to run, but only one app can hold the control connection to the earbuds at a time, so close the JBL Headphones app first.

## What you can control

Each model shows only the settings it has:

- Battery, and noise control: noise cancelling, Ambient Aware and TalkThru, with adaptive noise cancelling where the model has it.
- The equalizer for every model, with JBL's presets and a curve of your own on ten bands.
- Smart Audio & Video, 3D spatial sound, SafeSound, balance and Hi-Res audio, where the model has them.
- Touch controls on many models, including the Live Buds 3 and other Airoha-chip earbuds.

## JBL models

PairFree knows 112 JBL models. Models that have been tried on real earbuds: Live Buds 3.

JBL support is read from JBL's own app. A user's Live Buds 3 has been tried with PairFree.

The [full model list for JBL on GitHub](https://github.com/Omega-Destroyer/PairFree/blob/main/docs/devices/jbl.md) shows what works on each one. If your model is not marked as tried, a device report from you adds it: see [how to help](https://github.com/Omega-Destroyer/PairFree#help-add-your-model).

## Why use PairFree

- **No account and no internet.** The app has no internet permission, so it cannot go online.
- **One app for several brands.** The same screens for every brand, instead of one app per pair of earbuds.
- **Open source.** GPL-3.0; the protocol is documented in the repository with tests, so anyone can check what the app sends to the earbuds.
- **Small and quick,** about 3 MB, and reproducible: the published APK can be rebuilt from the source.

## Get PairFree

PairFree is on [F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/io.github.omegadestroyer.pairfree/) and on the [GitHub releases page](https://github.com/Omega-Destroyer/PairFree/releases/latest). It needs Android 12 or later.
