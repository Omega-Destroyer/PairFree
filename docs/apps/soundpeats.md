PairFree is a free, open-source Android and Windows app for SOUNDPEATS earbuds. It does what PeatsAudio, the official app, does, without an account or internet access.

PairFree does not need PeatsAudio (the SOUNDPEATS app) to run, but only one app can hold the control connection to the earbuds at a time, so close PeatsAudio (the SOUNDPEATS app) first.

## What you can control

Each model shows only the settings it has:

- Battery for each earbud and the case.
- Noise control: off, noise cancelling and transparency, with their scenes and levels where the model has them.
- The equalizer: the model's own presets and your own saved curves, and a hearing test that builds a curve for you.
- Game mode, LDAC and LE Audio, two devices at once, the ear tip fit test, find my earbuds and water drain.
- Touch controls and the settings only some models have, such as spatial audio, Dolby and dynamic bass.

## On Windows too

Almost none of these brands makes a Windows app, so on a PC their earbuds are plain Bluetooth audio. PairFree for Windows is one small portable program (no installer) with the same settings as the Android app, in a window made for a mouse and with a tray icon for the battery and quick switches. It needs Windows 10 (version 1809) or 11, 64-bit, with the earbuds paired in Windows' Bluetooth settings. SOUNDPEATS is covered.

## SOUNDPEATS models

PairFree knows 88 SOUNDPEATS models. Models that have been tried on real earbuds: Air5 Pro+, C30, H3, T3 Pro, TRUEFREE Clip C10.

SOUNDPEATS earbuds are the project's origin: the owner's H3 and Air5 Pro+ are tested in every feature, and users' reports cover the C30 and the TRUEFREE Clip C10.

If your model is not marked as tried, a device report from you adds it: see [how to help](https://github.com/Omega-Destroyer/PairFree#help-add-your-model).

## Why use PairFree

- **No account and no internet.** The app has no internet permission, so it cannot go online.
- **One app for several brands.** The same screens for every brand, instead of one app per pair of earbuds.
- **Open source.** GPL-3.0; the protocol is documented in the repository with tests, so anyone can check what the app sends to the earbuds.
- **Small and quick:** about 6 MB on Android and 16 MB on Windows, and reproducible: the published APK can be rebuilt from the source.
- **On Windows as well as Android,** where most of these brands have no app at all.

## Get PairFree

On Android, PairFree is on [F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/packages/io.github.omegadestroyer.pairfree/) and on the [GitHub releases page](https://github.com/Omega-Destroyer/PairFree/releases/latest); it needs Android 12 or later. On Windows 10 or 11, download `PairFree.exe` from the same releases page and run it.
