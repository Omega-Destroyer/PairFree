# 1MORE

PairFree is growing into one app for earbuds of several brands, with the same screens for each.
1MORE support is new and has not been tried with PairFree on any earbuds yet: nobody on the project
owns a pair, so every model relies on reports from people who do.

PairFree lists 1MORE earbuds and headphones among your paired devices by their Bluetooth name, as
1MORE's own app does, and talks to them over the same Bluetooth connection 1MORE's app uses. Close
1MORE's app first: only one app can hold that connection.

What it shows, where the model has it:
- **Home:** the battery (each earbud and the case, or one for headphones), noise control (off, noise
  cancelling, transparency), the kind of noise cancelling (adaptive, strong, mild or wind) and the kind
  of transparency (plain, pass-through or voice enhancement).
- **Sound:** the equalizer: the model's preset sounds and, on the models with one, a curve of your own from -3
  to 3 dB a band; spatial audio.
- **Controls:** what a double and a triple tap do, on each earbud or on both alike.
- **Device:** two devices at once, the connection mode (standard, game mode or LDAC), what happens when
  you take them out, sleep detection, voice commands, finding a lost earbud, and on the HQ20 hearing your
  own voice and the volume limit.

Not yet: the music and sleep sound styles.

Two models, the S52 and the S22Ultra, are Tuya AI earbuds: 1MORE's app reaches them through Tuya's
platform instead of its own connection, so PairFree does not list them.

1MORE support is written from 1MORE's own app (1MORE 2.0.0), read for its messages; none of its code is
in PairFree. What each model has comes from 1MORE's own product list, which 1MORE's app downloads; it
was fetched once for PairFree, and PairFree itself never contacts 1MORE. `docs/1more/PROTOCOL.md`
describes the protocol.

## Help wanted

A device report from any 1MORE model helps:
1. Connect the earbuds in PairFree.
2. Tap **Copy device report** on the Device tab.
3. Paste it into a new issue.

It lists the model and firmware and the last answer to each read PairFree sends, with no serial number,
address or name. Say which settings worked and which did nothing.
