# Bose

PairFree is growing into one app for earbuds of several brands, with the same screens for
each. Bose support is new and has not been tried with PairFree on any headphones or earbuds
yet: nobody on the project owns a pair, so every model relies on reports from people who do.

PairFree lists Bose headphones and earbuds among your paired devices when the phone shows one
of Bose's services for them, or when "Bose" is in the name. Each one reports its product when
PairFree connects and answers only for the settings it has, so a Bose model missing from the
table works the same way.

What it shows, where the model has it:
- **Home:** the battery (each earbud and the case, or one for headphones), the listening mode
  (Quiet, Aware and the modes you set up in Bose's app), the noise cancelling level, and on the
  QC35 noise cancelling off, low, high or wind.
- **Sound:** the tone control: bass, mid and treble, from -10 to +10.
- **Device:** two devices at once, pause when taken off, answering calls automatically, voice
  prompts, and how much of your own voice you hear during calls.

Not yet: buttons, the standby timer, spatial audio, changing the modes themselves, and anything
Bose's app does through Bose's cloud. PairFree never contacts Bose's servers, so whatever needs
Bose's sign-in stays out of reach: a model that refuses a change keeps its setting, and PairFree
shows what it holds.

Bose support is written from Bose's own app (Bose 13.3.4), read for its messages, and from
[bosectl](https://github.com/aaronsb/bosectl) by aaronsb (MIT), which controls Bose headphones
from a computer and has been tried on the models marked below. None of Bose's code is in
PairFree. `docs/bose/PROTOCOL.md` describes the protocol.

## Help wanted

A device report from any Bose model helps:
1. Connect the headphones or earbuds in PairFree.
2. Tap **Copy device report** on the Device tab.
3. Paste it into a new issue.

It lists the product and firmware and the last answer to each read PairFree sends, with no
serial number, address or name. Say which settings worked and which the device refused. A
recording of Bose's app does not help yet: PairFree cannot pick Bose's messages out of a
Bluetooth log, which has nothing to mark where one starts.
