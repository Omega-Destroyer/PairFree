# EarFun

PairFree is growing into one app for earbuds of several brands, with the same screens for each.
EarFun support is new and has not been tried with PairFree on any earbuds yet: nobody on the project
owns a pair, so every model relies on reports from people who do.

PairFree lists EarFun earbuds and headphones among your paired devices by their Bluetooth name, and talks
to them over the same Bluetooth connection EarFun's app uses. Close EarFun's app first: only one app can
hold that connection. EarFun's ATVEL models are set up in EarFun's app and are listed here too.

What it shows, where the model has it:
- **Home:** the battery (each earbud and the case, or one for headphones), noise control (off, noise
  cancelling, ambient sound), the kind of noise cancelling (such as adaptive, wind, balanced or levels 1
  to 4) and the kind of ambient sound.
- **Sound:** the equalizer: EarFun's preset curves and a curve of your own, 10 bands from -6 to 6 dB (6 bands
  from -10 to 10 dB on the Air S, ATVEL Tune S5 ProX and Free 2S); spatial audio. The earbuds cannot report
  their EQ, so PairFree shows the one this phone last set.
- **Controls:** what one, two and three taps and press and hold do on each earbud, the call gestures on the Air
  Pro 4+, 5 and Clip 2, turning the touch controls off, and on headphones what each button does.
- **Device:** two devices at once, game mode, which microphone takes calls, the voice prompt volume and language,
  a volume limit, auto power-off, pausing and answering calls when you take the earbuds out or put them in,
  finding a lost earbud, sound quality (LDAC, aptX, aptX Adaptive, aptX Lossless: the earbuds restart after a
  change), and restarting or turning the earbuds off, after you confirm.

Not yet: the voice prompt languages of the Qualcomm models, which EarFun's app downloads as voice packs, and the
models on chips whose own protocol PairFree does not cover yet (marked in the table). The speakers are left for
later.

EarFun support is written from EarFun's own app (EarFun Audio 20.1.61), read for its messages; none of its
code is in PairFree. Gadgetbridge's EarFun support was a check. `docs/earfun/PROTOCOL.md` describes the
protocol.

## Help wanted

A device report from any EarFun model helps:
1. Connect the earbuds in PairFree.
2. Tap **Copy device report** on the Device tab.
3. Paste it into a new issue.

It lists the model and firmware and the last answer to each read PairFree sends, with no serial number,
address or name. Say which settings worked and which did nothing.
