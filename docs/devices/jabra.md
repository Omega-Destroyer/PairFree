# Jabra

PairFree is growing into one app for earbuds and headphones of several brands, with the same screens for each. Jabra support is new and **has not been tried with PairFree on any product**:
nobody on the project owns one, so every model relies on reports from people who do. It is written from Jabra's own app (Sound+ 5.22.0), read for what it sends and what its bundled
product files list for each model and firmware; none of its code is in PairFree. [`docs/jabra/PROTOCOL.md`](../jabra/PROTOCOL.md) describes the protocol and
[`docs/jabra/SETTINGS.md`](../jabra/SETTINGS.md) lists every setting of every firmware snapshot.

PairFree lists the products below among your paired devices by their Bluetooth name ("Jabra Elite 75t") and talks to them over the standard serial port Sound+ uses (Jabra's own protocol, GNP).
Close Sound+ first: only one app can hold that connection. The product id and firmware version the earbuds report pick the snapshot of Sound+'s product file whose settings PairFree reads, so a
model shows what Sound+ shows for its firmware, and a row appears once the earbuds have answered its read.

What it shows, where the model and firmware have it:
- **Home:** the battery of each earbud and the case (which side is which is read from the battery message's addresses, not from a link event: unconfirmed); noise control (off, noise cancelling, HearThrough)
  on the three forms Jabra has used (the ambience command of the newer models, the older noise command, or only HearThrough's switch); the HearThrough and noise cancelling levels, the music mix, and the modes
  the button cycles through.
- **Sound:** the equalizer on the bands the earbuds describe, with Sound+'s six presets (each a percentage of the band's own maximum).
- **Controls:** each earbud's tap, double and triple tap for music, an incoming call and a call, with the actions Sound+ offers for that firmware.
- **Device:** sidetone and its level, the voice prompts and their language (among those on the earbuds), the automatic switch-off, pausing when taken off, on-head detection, call audio, wind noise reduction,
  automatic volume, Spotify's one tap button and spatial sound's mode, as the model's firmware lists them.
- The device report keeps the last answer to each read PairFree made.

The Elite 2, 3, 3 Active, 4, 4 Active, 5 and Connect 5t are driven through Qualcomm's GAIA, with Jabra's own plugins (battery, noise modes, HearThrough, sidetone, in-ear detection, the button,
leveled noise cancelling) and the equalizer of Qualcomm's music plugin; they show what those plugins answer. Not covered yet: MySound's hearing personalization, soundscapes, renaming (left out on purpose) and the
speakerphones and desk headsets that are not earbuds or headphones. [`docs/jabra/PROTOCOL.md`](../jabra/PROTOCOL.md) says what was checked.

## Help wanted

Jabra owners: **Copy device report** (Device tab) after connecting, and paste it into a new issue at https://github.com/Omega-Destroyer/PairFree/issues. It holds no serial number or address. Say what
PairFree showed and what worked or did not, above all which earbud the left and right battery levels belong to.
