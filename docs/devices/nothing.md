# Nothing and CMF

PairFree is growing into one app for earbuds of several brands, with the same screens for
each. Nothing and CMF support is new and has not been tried with PairFree on any earbuds or
headphones yet: nobody on the project owns a pair, so every model relies on reports from people
who do.

PairFree lists Nothing and CMF earbuds and headphones among your paired devices when the phone
shows Nothing's control service for them, or by their name. They report their product when
PairFree connects, and Nothing X's own feature table says what each model has.

What it shows, where the model has it:
- **Home:** the battery (each earbud and the case, or one for headphones), noise control (off,
  noise cancelling, transparency) and the noise cancelling strength (adaptive, high, mid, low).
  The Ear (stick) has a noise cancelling switch from firmware x.x.2 on.
- **Sound:** the equalizer: presets (balanced, more bass, more treble, voice) and a curve of your own
  over bass, mid and treble; bass boost and its
  level, spatial audio, and head tracking on the headphones.
- **Controls:** what each tap, double tap, triple tap and hold does, for each earbud; on the CMF Buds
  Pro 2 the case dial and on the Ear (3) the case button; on the headphones the control button, the
  roller's press and hold, and the CMF Headphone Pro's slider.
- **Device:** low lag mode, in-ear detection, the codec the earbuds offer (LDAC or LHDC on the
  models that have them), and making each earbud beep to find it.

Not yet: Nothing's advanced equalizer and the Dirac presets on CMF models, dual connection, the
ear tip fit test, and personalised noise cancelling and sound.

Nothing and CMF support is written from Nothing's own app (Nothing X 3.4.15), read for its
messages and its per-model feature table, and checked against
[nada](https://github.com/chukfinley/nada) by chukfinley and
[nadamas](https://github.com/ezvk/nadamas) by ezvk (both MIT), which control Nothing and CMF
earbuds from a phone and a computer and were used with the models marked below. None of
Nothing's code is in PairFree. `docs/nothing/PROTOCOL.md` describes the protocol.

## Help wanted

A device report from any Nothing or CMF model helps:
1. Connect the earbuds or headphones in PairFree.
2. Tap **Copy device report** on the Device tab.
3. Paste it into a new issue.

It lists the product and firmware and the last answer to each read PairFree sends, with no
serial number, address or name. Say which settings worked, which did nothing, and whether a
touch control you changed took effect: one tester of another app found a model that accepts a
new touch action and keeps doing the old one.

A recording of Nothing X helps more: it shows exactly what Nothing X sends for each setting,
which settles what PairFree has had to infer, such as the numbers of the equalizer presets.
Tap **Record from the official app** on the Device tab and follow the steps, using Nothing X as
the official app ([Record from the official app](../../README.md#record-from-the-official-app)).
