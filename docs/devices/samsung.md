# Samsung Galaxy Buds

PairFree is growing into one app for earbuds of several brands, with the same screens for
each. Galaxy Buds support is new and has not been tried on any earbuds yet: nobody on the
project owns a pair, so every model relies on reports from people who do.

PairFree lists every model below among your paired earbuds by its Bluetooth name. If you have
renamed your earbuds, it does not find them yet.

It shows the battery and noise control on the Home tab, and the equalizer on the Sound tab:
- **Presets:** Normal plus Samsung's five presets on every model.
- **Custom curve:** a nine-band curve on the Buds3 and later.

Every other setting the model has appears as its own row on the tab where it fits:
- **Touch controls:** gestures and what touch and hold does.
- **Ambient sound:** level, extra loud, voice focus and each earbud's level and tone.
- **Noise cancelling:** level, with one earbud, and voice detect.
- **Calls and connection:** call settings, seamless connection, voice wake-up and balance.
- **Fit test and Find my earbuds.**

Samsung's earbuds have no codec setting: the phone picks the codec.

Galaxy Buds support is built on [GalaxyBudsClient](https://github.com/timschneeb/GalaxyBudsClient)
by Tim Schneeberger, the open-source Galaxy Buds app, licensed GPLv3. The model list, each
model's features, the message format and every model's state layout come from its source. Its
tests supply the state replies the layouts are checked against. For the Galaxy Buds4 and Buds4
Pro, and the Buds On, H1 and T1 that GalaxyBudsClient does not have, PairFree follows Samsung's
own Galaxy Buds plugin instead, which also gives the Buds4 family's preset curves, firmware
version and the choice of what touch and hold switches between. `docs/samsung/PROTOCOL.md`
describes the protocol.

## Help wanted

A report from any Galaxy Buds model helps. The best is a recording of Galaxy Wearable
changing each setting: on PairFree's Device tab, tap **Record from the official app** and
follow its steps ([details](../../README.md#record-from-the-official-app)). It shows exactly
what Samsung's own app sends for each setting.

Quicker, a device report:
1. Connect the earbuds in PairFree.
2. Tap **Copy device report** on the Device tab.
3. Paste it into a new issue.

The device report holds the earbuds' whole state, as bytes and as PairFree read it. It
contains no serial number or address. Say which settings worked and which did not. Reports
from the Buds3 and later help most: their layouts have not been checked against any real
reply.
