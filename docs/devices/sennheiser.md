# Sennheiser

PairFree is growing into one app for earbuds and headphones of several brands, with the same screens for each. Sennheiser support is new and **has not been tried with PairFree on any product**:
nobody on the project owns one, so every model relies on reports from people who do. It is written from Sennheiser's own apps (Smart Control 4.9.4 and Smart Control Plus 1.6.3), read for what they send;
none of their code is in PairFree. [`docs/sennheiser/PROTOCOL.md`](../sennheiser/PROTOCOL.md) describes the protocols.

PairFree lists the products below among your paired devices by their Bluetooth name (or Sennheiser's own serial service) and talks to them over the same Bluetooth connection Smart Control uses. Close
Smart Control first: only one app can hold that connection.

What it shows, where the product answers (a model with a product file in Smart Control shows only what the file lists; the Momentum 5, Momentum True Wireless 5, HDB 630 and HDR 275 have none, so PairFree asks for
everything and shows what answers):
- **Home:** the battery of each earbud and the case; noise control (off, noise cancelling, transparency, as Smart Control does: turning one on turns the other off); anti-wind and adaptive noise
  cancelling; pause music in full transparency; the transparency level (a slider on the Accentum Plus and Momentum 4, three levels on the earbuds).
- **Sound:** the equalizer, in whichever of Sennheiser's three forms the product answers (five bands the earbuds hold, a five-band shelf set, or bass, mid and treble) with Smart Control's presets and
  bass boost; podcast mode; personalised sound on and off (and a confirmed reset); the earbuds are switched to their user equalizer mode when a curve is sent.
- **Controls:** the touch and button functions for each gesture of each earbud, as Smart Control's screen for the product offers them (the Accentum, Accentum Plus and Momentum 4 have fixed gestures), and a way back to the defaults.
- **Device:** answering calls automatically, comfort call, sidetone, on-head detection, smart pause, low latency mode, locking the touch controls, automatic power off, eco mode, battery protection,
  Bluetooth compatibility, aptX Lossless, tap sensitivity, voice prompts and tones, the codec, sample rate and where each earbud is worn; find my earbuds (a sound in each); the fit test; connecting and disconnecting
  the paired devices; Auracast (the earbuds look for broadcasts and listen to one); the Momentum Sport's heart rate and body temperature; a restart.
- The older headphones Smart Control opens over the serial port (CX 350BT, HD 250BT, HD 350BT, HD 450BT, HD 450SE, HD 458BT, IE 80S BT: GAIA v2; Momentum 3, PXC 550, PXC 550-II: Nautilus) show what their product files list,
  as the table says.
- The device report keeps the answer to each property PairFree asked for.

Not covered yet: the Bluetooth LE only products (the CX 200, CX 200 Sports, CX Plus and CX 500, and the CX 400BT, Momentum True Wireless 1 and 2 and CX 150) cannot be opened (they need a scan permission that is the owner's to allow
and Sennheiser's own bonding step), the Accentum Clip (Airoha) and the soundbars, personalised sound's listening test, Auracast broadcasts that need a code, deleting a paired device, and the equalizers of the IE 80S BT, HD 458BT
and the Nautilus headphones. [`docs/sennheiser/PROTOCOL.md`](../sennheiser/PROTOCOL.md) says what was read for each. PairFree never sends a factory reset, firmware update or the panic log erase.

## Help wanted

Sennheiser owners: **Copy device report** (Device tab) after connecting, and paste it into a new issue at https://github.com/Omega-Destroyer/PairFree/issues. It holds no serial number or address. Say what
PairFree showed and what worked or did not. The Momentum 5 and Momentum True Wireless 5 especially: PairFree knows them only from Smart Control Plus's product list.
