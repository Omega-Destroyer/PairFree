# Skullcandy

PairFree is growing into one app for earbuds and headphones of several brands, with the same screens for each. Skullcandy support is new and **has not been tried with PairFree on any product**: nobody on the project owns one,
so every model relies on reports from people who do. It is written from Skullcandy's own app (5.0.6) and the Bragi and Airoha SDKs inside it, read for what they send; none of their code is in PairFree.
[`docs/skullcandy/PROTOCOL.md`](../skullcandy/PROTOCOL.md) describes the protocols and [`docs/skullcandy/STATUS.md`](../skullcandy/STATUS.md) lists what PairFree does for each product.

Skullcandy's products speak five different protocols, which the app tells apart by the product, so PairFree does the same and each model shows what its own route carries:
- **Airoha earbuds and headphones over AT** (Skullcandy's serial service or the standard serial port): the battery, noise mode, the transparency level where the SDK lists it, and gaming mode; a second serial channel carries Airoha's own
  commands: multipoint, wear detection, the Crusher PLYR 720's bass boost, the strength of noise cancelling and transparency on the Crusher ANC 2, and the equalizer (the earbuds' presets, and a curve of your own on five, seven or ten bands).
- **Rail and Rail ANC:** Airoha RACE alone: the batteries (both earbuds and the case), noise cancelling or transparency with strength, multipoint, the equalizer, the buttons and voice control.
- **Method 360 and 540 ANC, Crusher 1080 ANC:** Skullcandy's protobuf RPC, without its optional secure session: noise modes with strength and profile, gaming mode, voice prompts and their language, sidetone, wear detection, the Crusher
  controls, spatial audio, multipoint, button actions and the equalizer; the Crusher 1080 ANC also has an Auracast receiver (scan, pick a broadcast, its code, stop).
- **Icon 180, Dime 540 ANC, Smokin' Buds 360 H and S, Push 180 Open (BES):** BES's TOTA protocol with its AES handshake: the battery, hearing mode and level, the equalizer, multipoint, gaming mode, sidetone, touch controls and button actions.
- **Crusher ANC, Crusher Evo, Indy Fuel, Indy Evo, Push Ultra and Crusher 540 Active (Qualcomm, Bluetooth LE):** the batteries, the Indy models' ambient mode and three equalizer presets, over the app's own frames.
- The equalizer on the Airoha models is read back, so the Sound tab shows what the earbuds hold; **Find with Tile** (22 products) opens Tile's app or page.

Close the Skullcandy app first: only one app can hold the connection. The models whose control channel is Bluetooth LE ask for the scan permission when you open one. Not covered: the four Session speakers, the three Terrain speakers
and Smokin' Buds 3 (the app shows no settings for the last four groups; speakers are held back), Mimi's personal sound (it signs in to Mimi's cloud), the optional secure session of the RPC models, and the settings of the Skullcandy
account (product registration, the Skull-iQ assistant). [`docs/skullcandy/PROTOCOL.md`](../skullcandy/PROTOCOL.md) says what was checked and what each reading rests on; several frames and replies are inferences from the app's code
and are marked as such there.
