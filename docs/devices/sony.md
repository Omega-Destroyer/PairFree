# Sony

PairFree is growing into one app for earbuds of several brands, with the same screens for
each. Nobody on the project owns Sony earbuds or headphones, so every model relies on reports from people who do: a user's report
from the WF-1000XM5 and a recording and confirmation from the WF-1000XM6 (its equalizer works) are the two so far.

PairFree lists Sony earbuds and headphones among your paired devices when the phone shows
Sony's control service for them, or by the model names below. Each model says itself what it
has when PairFree connects, so a Sony model missing from the table works the same way.

What it shows, where the model has it:
- **Home:** the battery (each earbud and the case, or one for headphones), and noise control:
  off, noise cancelling or ambient sound, with the ambient sound level, voice focus, wind noise
  reduction and speak-to-chat.
- **Sound:** the equalizer, with the model's presets and, where it takes one, a curve of your
  own over the bands it reports; Clear Bass; and DSEE upscaling.
- **Controls:** what each touch sensor or button does (noise control, volume, playback, track
  control, the voice assistant, or nothing), from the choices the model lists. A key set to Google
  Assistant, Alexa or Quick Access in Sony's app shows it and keeps it.
- **Device:** the Bluetooth connection mode (sound quality or a stable connection), automatic power
  off, pause when taken off, voice guidance and its volume, two devices at once (multipoint), the
  settings the earbuds describe themselves (the touch panel, wide area tap, sidetone, noise cancelling
  with one earbud and others), power saving, announcing the battery level, the power on and off
  sound, and turning them off.
- Also, where the model has them: the noise cancelling optimizer on Home, and on the Sound tab sound
  leakage reduction, voice content mode, cinema upmix, adaptive volume and the background music
  effect.

Also, from the app's own lists (0.12.0, none tried on real earbuds): the LE Audio switch and connection mode (the earbuds
reconnect, as the app warns), left and right balance correction, vibration, power saving on the older models, standby,
caring charge, stamina mode, the touch panel's light, auto play, auto volume, a maximum volume limit and battery safe mode
(an "i" beside a setting explains the ones whose effect is not obvious).

Not offered, with the reason in `docs/sony/PROTOCOL.md`: Adaptive Sound Control (the phone runs it from places and activity and
needs location permission), safe listening (a listening log the app stores), 360 Reality Audio, head gestures and head tracking
(they run on the headphones' motion sensor stream), the voice assistant's wake word and quick access (they need an assistant set up
on the phone), the call microphone test, the ear tip fitting test and the curves of Sony's creator sound effect (content from
Sony's server).

Sony support is written from Sony's own app (Sony | Sound Connect 13.3.0), read for its
messages, and checked against [Gadgetbridge](https://codeberg.org/Freeyourgadget/Gadgetbridge),
whose Sony support has been used with real headphones; its notes give the table sets below.
None of either app's code is in PairFree. `docs/sony/PROTOCOL.md` describes the protocol.

## Help wanted

A report from any Sony model helps. The best is a recording of Sony's app changing each
setting: on PairFree's Device tab, tap **Record from the official app** and follow its steps
([details](../../README.md#record-from-the-official-app)).

Quicker, a device report:
1. Connect the earbuds or headphones in PairFree.
2. Tap **Copy device report** on the Device tab.
3. Paste it into a new issue.

The device report lists the functions the model reports and the last message of each kind it
sent. It contains no serial number or address. Say which settings worked and which did not.
