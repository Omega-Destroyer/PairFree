# Soundcore (Anker) earbuds

PairFree is growing into one app for earbuds of several brands, with the same screens for
each. Soundcore support is in progress. PairFree lists every model below among your paired
earbuds and shows its battery, noise control, game mode, LDAC, two devices at once, touch
controls and touch tone, where the model has them, and the Sleep A20's sleep timer. It also sets
the equalizer, presets and custom curves, on every model with an EQ; the table says how far each
model's writes are confirmed. The Space A40 has been tried with PairFree by the project's owner,
the AeroClip and the Sleep A20 by users, the Sleep A20 with a recording of the Soundcore app; the
other settings come next.

Soundcore support is built on [OpenSCQ30](https://github.com/Oppzippy/OpenSCQ30) by Kyle
Scheuing, the open-source Soundcore app, licensed GPL-3.0-or-later like PairFree. The model
list, each model's features, the packet format, where each model puts each setting in its
replies, and the EQ correction come from OpenSCQ30's source, and its tests supply the replies
the layouts are checked against. Recordings from a real Space A40 (`data/soundcore/reports/`)
confirmed them, and showed that its case reports its battery in percent. The parsers of the
Soundcore app's last readable version (2.9.9) confirmed 15 of the layouts and gave 8 models
OpenSCQ30 lacks.
`docs/soundcore/PROTOCOL.md` describes the protocol.

<b>Tested</b> (3): tried on the earbuds with PairFree. <b>Checked</b> (41): PairFree reads every reply from the model that OpenSCQ30's tests hold, most of them from its users' bug reports, or reads the reply as the Soundcore app's own code does. <b>Not tested</b>: read from OpenSCQ30's code alone. <b>Report only</b>: the Soundcore app lists it, but nothing gives its layout yet; PairFree finds it and offers a device report, and shows no settings.
