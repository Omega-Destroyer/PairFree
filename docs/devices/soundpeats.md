# SOUNDPEATS earbuds

PairFree replaces PeatsAudio, the official app for SOUNDPEATS earbuds. Almost every model uses the
same protocol (`docs/soundpeats/PROTOCOL.md`), so PairFree connects to any of them and shows what
the earbuds answer. This page lists the models tried on real earbuds, every model in the official
app's table, and what has been confirmed on each. The [README](../../README.md#help-add-your-model)
says how to add yours.

## Equalizer bands

The sliders go from −6 to +6 dB in 1 dB steps, on top of the model's own tuning. 0 dB on
every band is the earbuds' stock sound (SOUNDPEATS Classic in PeatsAudio).

|          | SOUNDPEATS H3                                     | SOUNDPEATS Air5 Pro+                              |
|----------|---------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------|
| Bands, Hz | 50, 160, 350, 800, 1.6k, 2.8k, 4.6k, 8k, 12k, 17k | 32, 65, 180, 500, 800, 1.8k, 3.6k, 5.5k, 8k, 12k |
| Presets  | SOUNDPEATS Classic, Bass Boost, Bass Reduce, Treble Boost, Electronic, Pop, Classical, Rock, Folk, Book Whisper, HIFI, ACG | The same twelve names, with this model's own curves |

Every band's frequency, Q and gain, and every preset, are in `data/soundpeats/devices.json`. The two
models have different curves, so one model's EQ is never used for another.

## Other models

Almost every model the official app supports uses the same protocol, so PairFree
should connect to the other models in `data/soundpeats/devices.json` (all but the Capsule3 Pro)
and to newer ones, and show whatever they answer. None of them has been tested yet. The equalizer appears only once
someone has recorded the model's curves.

If you have one, see [Help add your model](../../README.md#help-add-your-model): one recording from
your phone adds its controls and its equalizer to the table.

The Capsule3 Pro is the exception. It uses a different protocol over Bluetooth LE,
documented in `docs/soundpeats/PROTOCOL.md` section 10, and PairFree does not support it yet.

## LDAC on Samsung phones

Turning LDAC on in PairFree (or PeatsAudio) makes the earbuds offer LDAC. The phone still picks
the codec it plays, and Samsung phones keep a switch of their own for it, per pair of earbuds:

1. Open **Settings > Connections > Bluetooth** (PairFree's **Bluetooth settings** button opens it).
2. Tap the gear next to the earbuds.
3. Under **Codecs**, turn on **LDAC**.

Without it the phone plays AAC, and forcing LDAC in Developer options is refused, as happened with
an Air5 Pro+ on a Galaxy S24 FE until the switch was turned on; SoundPEATS pointed to it. The same
phone had the switch on for the H3 already. Other phones may have a similar switch (on Pixel phones
it is called HD audio). Android does not let apps read which codec the
phone uses, so PairFree cannot tell whether the phone followed; it reminds you of the switch when
you turn LDAC on. On the Air5 Pro+, turning LDAC on also stops the earbuds offering aptX, so if
the phone's switch stays off the sound falls back to AAC.

Whether every pair of LDAC earbuds needs this switch, or only some, is not known yet. If you can
help, open an issue with your phone, its Android version, your earbuds, whether the switch was on
before you touched it, and which codec **Bluetooth Audio Codec** in Developer options shows with the
switch on and off.

Details: `docs/soundpeats/PROTOCOL.md` section 12.
