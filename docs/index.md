PairFree is a free, open-source Android app that replaces the brand-specific apps for wireless earbuds and headphones with one light, fast one. It talks to your earbuds directly over Bluetooth to set noise cancelling, the equalizer, touch controls and more. It has no account, no internet access and no ads.

It covers 644 models from 13 brands. Pick yours:

- [Open-source SOUNDPEATS app (a PeatsAudio alternative)](https://omega-destroyer.github.io/PairFree/open-source-soundpeats-app/): 88 SOUNDPEATS models
- [Open-source Soundcore app (for Anker Soundcore earbuds)](https://omega-destroyer.github.io/PairFree/open-source-soundcore-app/): 67 Soundcore models
- [Open-source Bose app (a Bose Music alternative)](https://omega-destroyer.github.io/PairFree/open-source-bose-app/): 22 Bose models
- [Open-source JBL Headphones app alternative](https://omega-destroyer.github.io/PairFree/open-source-jbl-app/): 112 JBL models
- [Open-source Sony headphones app (a Sound Connect alternative)](https://omega-destroyer.github.io/PairFree/open-source-sony-app/): 24 Sony models
- [Open-source Galaxy Buds app (without Galaxy Wearable)](https://omega-destroyer.github.io/PairFree/open-source-galaxy-buds-app/): 16 Samsung Galaxy Buds models
- [Open-source AirPods app for Android (noise control and settings)](https://omega-destroyer.github.io/PairFree/open-source-airpods-beats-app/): 30 AirPods and Beats models
- [Open-source Nothing X app alternative (Nothing Ear and CMF Buds)](https://omega-destroyer.github.io/PairFree/open-source-nothing-app/): 19 Nothing and CMF models
- [Open-source Pixel Buds app (for Android phones that are not Pixels)](https://omega-destroyer.github.io/PairFree/open-source-pixel-buds-app/): 5 Google Pixel Buds models
- [Open-source QCY app alternative](https://omega-destroyer.github.io/PairFree/open-source-qcy-app/): 194 QCY models
- [Open-source 1MORE app alternative](https://omega-destroyer.github.io/PairFree/open-source-1more-app/): 39 1MORE models
- [Open-source EarFun app alternative (EarFun Audio)](https://omega-destroyer.github.io/PairFree/open-source-earfun-app/): 26 EarFun models
- [Open-source Cambridge Audio Melomania app alternative](https://omega-destroyer.github.io/PairFree/open-source-cambridge-app/): 2 Cambridge Audio models

## What it controls

Each model shows only what it has: the battery of each earbud and the case, noise control (off, noise cancelling, transparency, adaptive) with its scenes and levels, the equalizer with the brand's presets and your own curves, touch controls, game mode, LDAC and LE Audio, two devices at once, the ear tip fit test, find my earbuds, a home screen widget and a status notification, and the settings only one brand has.

## Why PairFree

- **No account, no internet.** The app does not have the internet permission, so it cannot go online.
- **One app to replace a drawer full of them.** Every brand gets the same screens.
- **Open source** (GPL-3.0), with every protocol documented in the repository and tested.
- **Reproducible builds:** the published APK can be rebuilt from the source and checked byte for byte.
- **Grows with its users:** a device report from your earbuds adds your model for everyone ([how to help](https://github.com/Omega-Destroyer/PairFree#help-add-your-model); the people who have are thanked in [CONTRIBUTORS.md](https://github.com/Omega-Destroyer/PairFree/blob/main/CONTRIBUTORS.md)).

## Questions

### Is there an open-source app for my earbuds?

Yes, for the brands above. PairFree is one open-source app (GPL-3.0) that talks directly to the earbuds over Bluetooth, so one app can replace the Soundcore, Bose Music, JBL Headphones, Sony Sound Connect, Galaxy Wearable, Nothing X, PeatsAudio, QCY, 1MORE, EarFun and Melomania apps.

### Does it work without an account or internet?

Yes. The app does not have the internet permission, so it cannot go online, and there is nothing to sign up for. Nothing leaves your phone unless you share a device report or an equalizer profile yourself.

### Does it work with every model of a brand?

It lists every model its sources describe, and each model shows only the settings it has. Most models have not been tried on real earbuds yet: their support comes from the brands' own apps and from other open-source projects. Each brand's page says which models have been tried, and a device report from your own earbuds helps the next person.

### Can I use PairFree and the brand's own app together?

Not at the same time: only one app can hold the control connection to a pair of earbuds. Close the brand's own app first.

### Which phones does it need?

Android 12 or later. AirPods need Android 17, or one of the few phones on Android 16 that already open their settings channel.

### Where do I get it?

From F-Droid or from the GitHub releases page. Both are the same APK, signed with the same key.
